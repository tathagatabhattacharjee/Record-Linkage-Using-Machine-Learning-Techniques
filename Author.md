<h2>Tathagata Bhattacharjee</h2>
<B>Data Scientist | Population Health Research | Data Standardisation | Machine Learning | Record Linkage | Health Data Engineering</B>
<BR><BR>
My work focuses on developing reliable, scalable, and reproducible approaches for integrating heterogeneous population and health datasets.
<BR><BR>
A particular research focus is the application of machine learning to record linkage, supported by synthetic-data generation and controlled experiments that enable methodological development while reducing unnecessary exposure of sensitive individual-level information.
<BR><BR>
The objective is to support the transformation of fragmented longitudinal and clinical information into high-quality, interoperable, and research-ready datasets for population-health and epidemiological research.
